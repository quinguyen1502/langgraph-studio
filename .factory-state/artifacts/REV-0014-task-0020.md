## Open issues / limits

- At-least-once only: the node in flight during a crash re-runs; fan-out points and imported workflows have no checkpoint and restart.
- A worker that lost its lease can double-execute (record is an upsert; last writer wins).
- The root span of a crashed segment is never exported (its node spans are).
- Bell polls every 30 s (no push); e-mail is plain text, no per-user preferences.
